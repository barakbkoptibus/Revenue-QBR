Math (transparent, reproducible)

GRR = (Opening − Churn − Downsell) / Opening = ($36.49M − $2.20M − $0.34M) / $36.49M = 93.0%
NRR = (Opening − Churn − Downsell + Upsell) / Opening = ($36.49M − $2.20M − $0.34M + $2.08M) / $36.49M = 98.7%
Net Live ARR motion = Closing − Opening = $37.07M − $36.49M = +$581K
Why this matters for the reconciliation: The DWH-computed 93.0% GRR is 1.1 pp higher than the FP&A-reported 91.9%. Tiny in pp terms, but it changes the FY26 retention story. The cycle-table approach surfaces both downgrade and upgrade explicitly, which is what makes the Egged-style account-level netting visible.
