SO WHAT 10 / 10

Three things that matter for Q2

01 · WIN RATE
Fix the denominator
12% win rate on $25K+ deals is below our historical 32%. The issue is qualified deals slipping or being lost, not pipeline volume. NA losses (RTA, MARTA, FTA) need a post-mortem before Q3 planning.
02 · Q2 EXECUTION
$2.4M to land
At $60K closed against $2.9M target with $4.7M qualified, we need ~62% conversion in 35 days. Tide AS ($1.18M) closing on Jun 30 is the single biggest swing factor for Q2.
03 · RENEWALS HYGIENE
Close the data gap
$687K renewed in Snowflake vs $1.86M in the manual deck means our successor-linkage is incomplete. This is fixable with SFDC discipline and would unlock automated GRR/NRR tracking in ThoughtSpot.
